# Joint NSDC × InfoMatrix Homepage — Corrected Design

This supersedes `2026-09-25-infomatrix-homepage-design.md` where they conflict. The homepage represents both National Student Data Corps and DJS InfoMatrix. It is not a replacement for either official site. Only this homepage is in scope.

## Composition

1. A quiet first screen: actual InfoMatrix and NSDC identity marks, a plain-language headline, a short explanation of the joint learning/building space, and clear routes into the page. No cloud video, beam, canvas playground, or showy 3D object in the hero. The background is near-black with a soft ultraviolet/ion glow and sparse fine geometry.
2. A prominent interactive lab immediately below: restore the existing clustering/regression/residuals canvas. Visitors can switch modes, click to add observations, and reset. This is the homepage's signature interaction.
3. A balanced explanation of each organization and what it contributes, with links to their official sites. Avoid inventing program details or claiming an enrollment mechanism that does not exist here.
4. Interactive domains and selected real project routes, followed by clear learning/community pathways and a simple footer.

## Visual language

Use the Laser Spectrum palette, but avoid all-uppercase headline stacks and an excess of monospaced labels. The official InfoMatrix site informs the proportion of black space to saturated violet/blue, with limited color concentrated in intentional areas. Main reading typography is a confident geometric sans; the serif is at most a light editorial accent. Localize the public identity marks so they remain visible if the official websites are unavailable.

Use restrained React Bits/Aceternity-inspired spotlight/tilt treatment in lower sections only. The lab is a real interaction, not decorative art. Motion must pause or simplify for reduced-motion preferences. No fake statistics, invented affiliations, or unsupported timing claims.

## Acceptance

- At desktop and mobile widths, the hero is simple and legible, displays both identities, and contains no cloud/video/beam.
- The lab appears below the hero and all three modes plus reset work.
- Lower sections retain purposeful interaction and links to both organizations.
- Build succeeds; keyboard navigation, contrast, and reduced-motion behavior are checked.
